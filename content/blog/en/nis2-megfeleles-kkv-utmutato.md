The EU NIS2 (Network and Information Security Directive 2) directive aims to ensure a high level of cybersecurity across critical and essential sectors in member states.

In Hungary, obligations for affected organizations are regulated under national cybersecurity legislation.

## Who is affected?

The regulation applies to medium and large enterprises in key sectors including:
- Energy and Transport
- Healthcare and Drinking Water
- Digital Infrastructure and IT Services (MSP, MSSP)
- Pharmaceuticals and Chemical Industry
- Food Production and Logistics

## Key Obligations

1. **Risk Assessment & Security Measures**: Regular vulnerability scans and implementation of defensive measures.
2. **Incident Reporting**: Mandatory reporting of major security incidents to authorities within 24 hours (initial) and 72 hours (detailed).
3. **Business Continuity**: Establishing backup strategies and Disaster Recovery Plans (DRP).
4. **Supply Chain Security**: Assessing cybersecurity risks of IT vendors and subcontractors.
